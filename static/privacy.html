Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 • Effective immediately

Privacy Guarantees at a Glance

  • Zero Voice Recording: SyncInk Radio never captures, records, or analyzes Discord voice channel conversations. Audio playback only transmits outbound media streams.
  • Minimal Data Footprint: Only IDs and preferences needed for playback queues, server configuration, radio favorites, and Audia AI taste customization are stored.
  • Direct User Control: Enable private listening mode, unblock/block artists, unlink Spotify, or clear your entire history directly from the dashboard or Discord commands.
  • Never Sold: Listener data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertising networks.

1. Scope & Service Identity

SyncInk Radio ("we", "us") operates the SyncInk Radio Discord Bot (Application ID: 1500567606648897819), the Audia AI Recommendation Engine, and the SyncInk Radio Web Dashboard (syncink.site). This policy explains what data is processed and how you control it.

2. Information We Process

  • Discord Identifiers: Discord User ID, username, display avatar hash, Guild (Server) ID, and target voice/text channel IDs when invoking commands or signing in via Discord OAuth2.
  • Queue & Guild Preferences: Configured guild music channels, default/max volume levels, 24/7 persistence toggles, saved favorites, and user-created playlists.
  • Listening History & Audia AI Taste Profile: Requested tracks, played radio stations, likes/dislikes, skip signals, and blocked artists used to personalize your History, Analytics, and AutoPlay queue (disabled immediately when Private Listening mode is active).
  • Spotify Account Linking (Optional): When linking Spotify via OAuth2 PKCE, authorization tokens and selected public playlist IDs are stored strictly to import playlists upon request. We never access passwords or billing credentials.

3. Voice Channel Audio Policy

SyncInk Radio does not record or process incoming user voice audio. The voice connection transmits encoded Opus frames exclusively outbound into your connected voice channel.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Discord Inc.: Subject to the Discord Privacy Policy.
  • Radio-Browser Community API: Used to resolve live public radio broadcast directories and country streams.
  • Spotify Web API: Governed by the Spotify Privacy Policy.
  • Public Media Metadata Providers: Used to resolve track titles, artists, artwork, and durations for requested audio streams.

5. Your Controls & Data Erasure

  • Private Listening Mode: Toggle on in the dashboard preferences to pause recording new entries to Listening History.
  • Clear History: Use the Clear History button in the Listening History tab or clear database/history entries anytime.
  • Spotify Unlink: Disconnect Spotify from your Library tab or via Discord slash commands at any time.
  • Full Data Removal: Contact our support team on the Official Discord Support Server to request complete deletion of all records linked to your Discord User ID.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy or data requests? Join our official community: https://discord.gg/PmxfJwmQuB
