Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about audio playback, worldwide radio, Audia AI, and Spotify linking.

In the Web Dashboard, open Audia AI Studio and scroll to Blocked Artists & Exclusions, then click Unblock next to the artist name. On Discord, run /audia and click the Unblock Artist button to choose from your blocked artists directly.
Audia AI learns from your completed listens, skips, Like/Dislike feedback buttons on the Discord player card, and manual style/language choices inside /audia or Audia AI Studio.
Run /radio in Discord to browse countries interactively, pass a country directly with /radio country: India, or open the Radio & Discover tab on the web dashboard to browse 30,000+ global stations.
